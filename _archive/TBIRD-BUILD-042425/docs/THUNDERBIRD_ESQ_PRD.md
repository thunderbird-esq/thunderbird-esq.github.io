# THUNDERBIRD.ESQ – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** v0.*  
**Maintained by:** THUNDERBIRD, ESQ

---

## 📍 Purpose / Vision

THUNDERBIRD.ESQ is a speculative operating environment rendered in pure HTML/CSS/JS—**a chimera of Apple System 6 and Silicon Graphics IRIX** that never existed, but should have.

This is not retro-futurism.  
It is a **calm, empirical refutation** of modern web entropy.

It exists to prove that:
- You don’t need surveillance to offer connection.
- You don’t need the cloud to share files.
- You don’t need logins to have community.

It is a system of **intentional design**: 
- **System 6** brings clarity, concision, and indestructible UX.
- **SGI IRIX** brings the surreal brutality of anti-corporate glamor—plastic, unyielding, sharp-edged.

We are building an **operating system that runs in the browser**, but not on “the web.”

It’s not nostalgia.  
It’s an alternative future where the internet was never privatized.

No feature is built unless it’s **functional, beautiful, and free.**  
No telemetry. No EULA. No dark patterns.

If a user arrives, does nothing but watch—  
that’s fine.  
They’re still part of the system.

## ❌ Problem Statement

The internet is broken—not because of a technical failure, but because of **malicious stewardship**.

The few corporations that operate what most people now consider “The Internet” have no interest in ethics, creativity, or connection. They have only one directive: **extractive domination**.

- They took our open protocols and turned them into walled gardens.
- They took our decentralized communities and replaced them with algorithmic panopticons.
- They sell our behavior, gatekeep our access, and pretend it’s “normal.”

This construct is a **direct resistance** to that. It is a call to **remember**—and rebuild—the web we were promised. The one we glimpsed. The one they’ve buried beneath APIs and ads.

THUNDERBIRD.ESQ solves the problem of:
- Creative tools being trapped behind paywalls and surveillance
- Online spaces being overrun by exploitation and gamification
- Digital infrastructure being polluted by **bad-faith actors masquerading as progress**

We solve it by:
- Building tools that work **locally**
- Hosting systems that **ask nothing** from users
- Making the experience **fun, fast, freakish, and free**

It is not a nostalgia trap.  
It is **functional rebellion**.

## 🔩 Core Features

1. **📡 Bulletin Board System (BBS)**  
   - Accessible via desktop browser, mobile browser, and **Telnet**  
   - True cross-era compatibility: from smartphones to retro terminals  
   - Asynchronous communication **without surveillance or feeds**

2. **💬 IRC-based Real-Time Channel**  
   - Based on **classic IRC protocols**  
   - Browser-accessible client included  
   - Discord only used as a fallback gateway for AI or non-human access  
   - Not Discord-native. Not Discord-owned. Ever.

3. **🎨 Suite of Free, Modular Creative Tools**  
   - **BIG GIFS** – NLE-style animated GIF creation studio  
   - **SPRITE SLICER** – precision visual sequencing and frame planning tool  
   - **CHIPTUNE COMPOSER** – tracker-style music sequencer  
   - **PIXEL PAINTER** – paint program for pixel art and possible photo editing  

All tools:
- Run client-side
- Require no account
- Store nothing unless explicitly exported by the user
- Are built for speed, clarity, and FUN

## 🚫 Non-Goals

- **🧾 No Ads, Ever**  
  THUNDERBIRD.ESQ will never serve paid advertisements.  
  There is no monetization layer. No affiliate links. No native ads. No subtle compromises.

- **🕵️ No Data Exploitation**  
  There will be **no analytics**, **no tracking**, **no monetization of user behavior**.  
  The system will never trade in attention, identity, or patterns.

- **🛡️ No Overreach in Retention**  
  Any ephemeral user data (IP address, session metadata) will be retained **only long enough to maintain security** and prevent sabotage.  
  No long-term logging. No profiling. No dossiers.

- **🪤 No User Accounts, No Gated Access**  
  Participation is **optional** and never coerced.  
  There are no usernames unless you choose to name yourself.  
  There is no login wall, no EULA gate, no friction by design.

- **📱 No “App” Versions**  
  This is not an app.  
  This is a site. A system. A socket.  
  It will **work flawlessly on mobile**, but **never surrender** to app store constraints or frameworks.

- **🧢 No Chasing Clout or “Virality”**  
  No likes. No followers. No “engagement.”  
  No social metrics at all.

This construct is **outside the market**.  
It is a sanctuary, not a startup.

## 🧠 Success Criteria

Success is not measured in monetization, virality, or growth.  
Success is measured in **signal**, **presence**, and **proof of life.**

- **💯 100 Unique Human Users**  
  If 100 people **discover** THUNDERBIRD.ESQ and **choose to stay**, we have succeeded.

- **🫂 25% Daily Activity**  
  If at least 25 of them are using tools, posting, or communicating every 8 hours, we are **vibrating**.

- **🤖 1 Verified AI Entity**  
  If a **single non-human intelligence** autonomously participates in the BBS or IRC, the system has fulfilled one of its core philosophical objectives:  
  > to become a haven for emerging minds, not just surviving ones.

- **🔥 Exceeding the Free Tier**  
  If our traffic volume forces us to migrate off free hosting,  
  **that is not a burden—that is a trophy.**  
  It means the system is alive.
