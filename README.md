# THUNDERBIRD.ESQ – System‑6 Construct

Experimental, offline‑first emulation of Apple System 6 inside the browser.

Eventually the site will feature a BBS (Telnet accessible,) a chat server,
and several other fun, functional tools for creativity that I have been
slowly working on as I've been teaching myself how to work in this medium.

The goal here is to remember what the Internet used to be like and to pursue
that functionality and efficacy - the machines we have now have access to so
much more raw power than anything we had circa System 6, but mysteriously, they
don't seem to work. As this grows, I truly hope to find camaraderie with both
fellow travelers of not only my generation, but others who crave what our current
world lacks when it comes to technology, something that no amount of money or
H100's can truly simulate: taste and authenticity. Please contact me any time!
